# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 2026  
**Entity:** CAn Beverages Inc.  

## 1. Information Collection & Usage
We collect information you provide directly to us when registering for priority drop notifications, placing sample orders, or contacting our concierge team. This includes your email address, shipping destination, and flavor preferences.

## 2. Zero Third-Party Data Monetization
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party data brokers or marketing syndicates under any circumstances. Data is utilized strictly to fulfill orders and notify you of private product drops.

## 3. Data Protection & GDPR / CCPA Compliance
You have the right to request access to, rectification of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing privacy@can-beverages.com.

## 4. Cookies & Local Storage
We use essential local session storage to remember your preloader state and consent preferences. No invasive tracking cookies are placed without explicit opt-in consent.
